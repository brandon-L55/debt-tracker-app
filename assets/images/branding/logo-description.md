# GotchuLatr — Logo & Brand Description

## 1. Overview

The GotchuLatr logo is a rounded-square app mark featuring a bold, custom white **“G”** that curls inward to cradle a friendly **checkmark** at its center. It sits on a vibrant diagonal gradient that sweeps from violet-purple into pink and warm coral. The result is approachable, energetic, and instantly legible at any size — a mark that feels more like a friend than a finance company.

## 2. The Custom “G” and Checkmark

The **“G”** is drawn as a single, confident stroke with soft rounded terminals. Rather than closing into a tight loop, its tail opens slightly and turns inward — a subtle visual cue of reaching out, including someone, _having their back_.

Nested inside that opening is a clean **checkmark**, the universal symbol for _done, settled, paid, confirmed_. Together they carry the core promise of the product: every debt tracked is a debt that gets resolved. The “G” represents the person and the relationship; the check represents the moment a balance is squared away. One holds the other — exactly the dynamic the app supports between friends.

## 3. The Purple → Pink → Coral Gradient

The background is a smooth diagonal gradient flowing from a deep **violet-purple** in the upper-left, through **magenta-pink**, into a warm **coral** in the lower-right.

- **Purple** signals trust, intelligence, and a touch of premium polish — it reassures users that their money matters are handled with care.
- **Pink** brings warmth, friendliness, and social energy — this is an app used _between people_, not a cold ledger.
- **Coral** adds optimism and approachability, keeping the whole mark feeling light, modern, and youthful.

The blend deliberately avoids traditional “money green” and corporate blue, positioning GotchuLatr as a casual, human, social product rather than a banking tool.

## 4. Connection to the Name “GotchuLatr”

The name is playful, conversational shorthand for **“I got you, [pay you] later.”** It’s the exact phrase friends say when someone covers a bill.

- **“Gotchu”** — _I’ve got you covered_ — is embodied by the protective, enclosing curve of the **“G”** wrapping around the check.
- **“Latr”** — the casual, texting-style spelling — is matched by the relaxed, friendly tone of the rounded shapes and bright palette.

The checkmark closes the loop on the promise: _I got you now, you’ll settle later, and we’ll mark it done._ The logo is a visual handshake on a casual IOU.

## 5. Brand Personality

- **Friendly** — soft rounded forms, warm coral and pink, a conversational name.
- **Trustworthy** — the steady purple base and the reassuring checkmark signal reliability and follow-through.
- **Modern** — a flat, gradient-forward app mark in step with contemporary mobile design.
- **Social** — built for groups of friends, roommates, and family; the palette and tone feel like a social app, not a bank.
- **Simple** — a single letter and a single check communicate the entire value proposition with zero clutter.

## 6. Suggested Use Cases

- **App icon** — the primary home for the mark; the rounded square is already iOS/Android ready.
- **Splash screen** — center the “G + check” on the full gradient (or a solid violet) while the app loads.
- **Website favicon** — the bold white “G” holds up clearly at 16×16 and 32×32.
- **App Store / Play Store icon** — high-contrast and distinctive in a crowded grid of competitors.
- **Marketing materials** — social posts, onboarding screens, stickers, and referral cards; the gradient doubles as a brand background, and the checkmark works as a standalone accent motif.

## 7. One-Paragraph Brand Version

> The GotchuLatr logo pairs a bold, custom white “G” with a friendly checkmark nestled inside it, set on a vibrant gradient that flows from purple through pink to coral. The enclosing “G” captures the app’s core promise — _I gotchu_ — while the checkmark marks every shared debt as settled and done. Warm, social, and modern, the mark trades corporate finance cues for a human, friendly feel that speaks directly to friends, family, students, and anyone splitting expenses and squaring up casual IOUs.

## 8. Technical Visual Description

**Format / Shape**

- Rounded-square (squircle) app icon with generously rounded corners.
- Centered symbol composed of two elements: a custom **“G”** and an enclosed **checkmark**.

**Symbol**

- Single-weight **“G”** with thick, even strokes and rounded (round-cap) terminals.
- Open aperture on the lower-right of the “G”, with the tail curving inward.
- A bold **checkmark** sits in the optical center, drawn in the same weight and round-cap style for visual unity.
- Both symbol elements are solid **white (#FFFFFF)** for maximum contrast.

**Background — Diagonal Gradient (≈135°, top-left → bottom-right)**

- Violet-purple: approx. **#8034DA**
- Magenta / mid-purple: approx. **#A636BE**
- Pink: approx. **#E45089**
- Coral: approx. **#F45E59**

> Suggested CSS: `linear-gradient(135deg, #8034DA 0%, #A636BE 40%, #E45089 75%, #F45E59 100%)`

**Style**

- Flat, modern, gradient-forward — no skeuomorphism, no drop shadows on the symbol.
- High contrast between the white mark and the saturated background ensures legibility from large hero placements down to favicon scale.
- Geometry is soft and rounded throughout (corners, strokes, terminals) to reinforce the friendly, approachable personality.

**Clear space & scaling**

- Maintain padding of at least 12–15% of the icon’s width around the symbol so it never crowds the rounded edges.
- The white mark remains the consistent hero across all backgrounds; if placed off the gradient, use the full-color gradient version rather than recoloring the “G”.
